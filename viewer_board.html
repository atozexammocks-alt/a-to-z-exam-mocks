Teach directly on your PDF

Open a PDF from your device or paste a publicly accessible Google Drive link. Draw on pages, insert whiteboard/blackboard pages between them, and save your annotations locally.

Drive files must be shared so the viewer can access them. If a Drive link fails, upload the PDF from your device. Use Crop → Board to move a question image to a fresh whiteboard and solve beneath it. Save session exports the PDF (when loaded), crops, boards and annotations together.
